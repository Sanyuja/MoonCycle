✦About Lunarly✦

Lunarly is a cycle-aware planning studio — it reads your body's rhythm and turns it into daily guidance for movement, work, food, and rest.

Two editions live here: Rhythm Studio, for natural-cycle women who train and perform, and CycleWise, for women navigating menopause who want energy-aware planning instead of period tracking.

✦

Archetypes

Natural cycle — phase-by-phase guidance across menstrual, follicular, ovulatory, and luteal days.

Menopause — a daily energy check-in (high / moderate / low) drives the same kind of guidance, with no cycle-day math.

✦How it works✦
  • Set your rhythm: natural cycle or menopause.
  • Tell Lunarly your main movement and brain practices.
  • Optionally upload health/habits CSVs.
  • Use daily/weekly guidance to plan movement, work, fasting, and rest.

Privacy basics: your data never leaves your browser — CSVs are parsed locally, nothing is uploaded or stored on a server.
